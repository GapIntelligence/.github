# OpenBrand

Market intelligence for brands and retailers. OpenBrand connects market share, consumer behavior, competitive pricing, promotions, and economic insights to help businesses understand their markets and make informed decisions.

[Explore OpenBrand](https://openbrand.com) · [Sign in](https://app.openbrand.com) · [Service status](https://status.openbrand.com) · [Trust Center](https://trust.openbrand.com)

## Public projects

[**DAR Schema**](https://github.com/GapIntelligence/DAR-Schema) defines the Data Archive Request format, an extension of HAR for web collection records and extraction results. Explore the schema, examples, and project documentation in the repository.

Other public repositories include infrastructure forks and archived libraries. See each repository's README, license, and archive status for its purpose and usage.
